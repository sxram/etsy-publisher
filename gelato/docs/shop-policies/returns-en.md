# Returns and cancellation – standard physical products

Prepared 18 October 2026. Draft for non-personalised T-shirts and printed posters. Not for digital downloads or individually personalised goods.

## Statutory cancellation rights (EU/EEA and UK)

You may cancel your purchase without giving a reason within 14 days after you, or someone you nominate other than the carrier, receive the goods. For several goods ordered together and delivered separately, the period starts when the last item is received.

To cancel, send a clear statement, for example by email or post, to:

Stefan Marx  
Friedrichsberger Str. 5  
10243 Berlin  
Germany  
Email: stefanmarx.de@gmail.com

You may use the form below, but this is optional. Sending your cancellation before the deadline is sufficient.

For a full cancellation, we refund all payments, including our least expensive standard delivery option. Additional costs for a more expensive delivery option you chose are not refunded. We refund within 14 days of receiving your cancellation, using the original payment method unless expressly agreed otherwise, with no refund fee. We may withhold the refund until we receive the goods or evidence that you sent them back, whichever happens first.

Send the goods to the address above without undue delay and within 14 days of telling us you are cancelling. Posting them within that period is sufficient. You pay the direct return shipping costs. You are responsible for diminished value only where it results from handling beyond what is necessary to establish the goods' nature, characteristics and functioning. Original packaging is not required to exercise your statutory cancellation rights.

## Voluntary worldwide extension to 30 days

For our non-personalised physical products, we extend the deadline for notifying us of a return to 30 days after receipt. Send the goods back within 14 days of notifying us. The same refund and return conditions described above apply. This voluntary promise applies worldwide. Mandatory local consumer rights remain unaffected, including rights relating to faulty or incorrectly supplied goods.

We do not offer voluntary direct exchanges. For a different size or colour, return the item and place a new order. Statutory rights to replacement of faulty goods are unaffected.

## Damaged or incorrect goods

Please contact us promptly through Etsy or email if an item arrives damaged, misprinted or incorrect. Photos of the goods and packaging help us investigate, but are not a blanket condition of statutory remedies. Please contact us before paying to return faulty goods so we can arrange the appropriate solution. Statutory rights and time limits remain unaffected.

Returns go to Stefan Marx in Berlin, not to Gelato or the printing facility. Prior approval is not required to exercise statutory cancellation rights.
